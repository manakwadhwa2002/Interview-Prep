# DSA — NeetCode 150

This folder contains my solutions and practice for the **NeetCode 150** DSA roadmap.

The goal is to build strong problem-solving fundamentals by systematically covering the most important data structures and algorithms commonly asked in technical interviews.

## 📚 Topics Covered

The problems are organized according to the NeetCode 150 categories:

1. **Arrays & Hashing**
2. **Two Pointers**
3. **Sliding Window**
4. **Stack**
5. **Binary Search**
6. **Linked List**
7. **Trees**
8. **Tries**
9. **Heap / Priority Queue**
10. **Backtracking**
11. **Graphs**
12. **Advanced Graphs**
13. **1-D Dynamic Programming**
14. **2-D Dynamic Programming**
15. **Greedy**
16. **Intervals**
17. **Math & Geometry**
18. **Bit Manipulation**

## 🎯 Goals

* Build strong DSA fundamentals.
* Understand the underlying patterns rather than memorizing solutions.
* Improve problem-solving and analytical thinking.
* Prepare for coding interviews.
* Revisit difficult problems regularly and optimize solutions over time.

## 🧠 Approach

For each problem, the focus is on:

1. Understanding the problem.
2. Identifying the underlying DSA pattern.
3. Coming up with a brute-force approach where applicable.
4. Improving it to an optimal solution.
5. Analyzing **time and space complexity**.
6. Implementing a clean and readable solution.
7. Revisiting problems that required significant help.

## 🔄 Revision Strategy

Solving a problem once is not the end goal. Problems that are difficult or required hints will be revisited periodically.

The focus during revision is to be able to:

* Recognize the pattern quickly.
* Explain the approach without looking at the solution.
* Implement it from scratch.
* State the time and space complexity.
* Identify possible edge cases.
